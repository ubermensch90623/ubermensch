# Weekly Synthesis Prompt

> 매주 월요일 아침 15분. 캘린더에 블록 등록 권장.
> Claude Code에 붙여넣기.

---

Read my entire Obsidian vault. Focus on everything added or modified in the
last 7 days. Also re-read `CLAUDE.md` for current Active Projects and Stuck-on
sections — these may have shifted since last week.

I want four things, in order:

## EMERGING THESIS

What idea am I building toward without having stated it explicitly yet? What
position is forming in my thinking?

This is the most important section. Look at what I've been:
- Capturing (inbox/, notes/)
- Synthesizing (ideas/)
- Returning to (which MOCs grew the most?)
- Skipping (what topics did I plan to read but didn't?)

State the emerging thesis in 1-2 sentences. Be specific enough that I would
either agree strongly or push back hard.

## CONTRADICTIONS

What have I saved recently that contradicts something I believed before? Show
me both sides from my own notes.

Format:
> **Earlier belief** (from [[note-X]], date): ...
> **Recent challenge** (from [[note-Y]], date): ...
> **Which side is winning in your synthesis?**

## KNOWLEDGE GAPS

Based on what I am reading and thinking about, what am I clearly **not**
reading that I should be? What perspective is missing?

Be specific:
- A specific author, book, or paper I should read
- A specific opposing view I'm avoiding
- A specific domain that's adjacent to my obsession but I keep skipping

## ONE ACTION

Given everything in this vault, what is the **single** highest-leverage thing
I could do or think about this week? Not 3 things. Not a list. One.

Constraints:
- Must be doable this week (not "in Q3")
- Must be specific (not "read more about X")
- Must connect to my Active Project or top obsession

---

Save the synthesis to `/notes/weekly/{YYYY-MM-DD}-synthesis.md`.

Use this structure:

- **Now**: from [[note-Y]], "..."
- **Verdict**: ...

---

규칙:
- Be direct. Challenge me. Do not summarize what I already know
- 동기부여성 답변 금지
- "잘하고 있다"는 사회적 윤활제 금지
- 노트가 부족하면 솔직히 "이번 주는 캡처가 적어 의미 있는 thesis 없음"이라고 말할 것
- ONE ACTION이 정말 하나여야 함 — "그리고", "또는" 금지
